## Headline findings

- **H2 SUPPORTED**: across 15+ products, zero accept weather, street crossings, or safety as routing constraints, and zero connect route capability to training state. Strava Suggested Routes is the closest (distance presets, flat/hilly, surface, popularity-ranked, not personalized).
- **H3 SUPPORTED with a caveat**: the plan wedge is crowded and consolidating; the route wedge is structurally open and demand-validated at three price points. Caveat: the wedge acquires better than it monetizes alone; revenue lives in the adaptive layer.
- **Kill-shot threat:** Strava connecting Runna's plan engine to its route generation. Estimated 50 to 60 percent likelihood of a v1 within 18 months, 20 to 30 percent for full constraint depth. Working window: 12 to 18 months. Watch item: Instant Workouts (Jan 2026) already links basic routes to workouts (noted in Phase 4).
- **Data enclosure:** Strava's API bans AI use of its data; Garmin's developer program is paused. Personalization must be HealthKit-first (Android: Health Connect in dual-platform work); the moat must be proprietary context data.
- **Do not compete on:** community/social graph, trail content libraries, coaching brand, hardware, price-zero basics, multi-sport breadth.
