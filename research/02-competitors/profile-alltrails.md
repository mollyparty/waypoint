# Competitor Profile: AllTrails

Version-Timestamp: 2026-07-30 14:30:00 UTC-4

AllTrails is the world's largest trail discovery platform (95 million plus users, 500,000 plus verified trail maps) and in May 2025 launched the Peak tier ($79.99 per year) with AI-assisted custom routes, predictive trail conditions, and community heatmaps. Its AI route features modify existing routes (shorter, less steep, more scenic, reversed) rather than generating routes on demand from training constraints, and everything snaps only to marked trails, paths, and roads. The product is fundamentally trail and hike centric: runners are an acknowledged but secondary audience, and urban doorstep running is outside its design center.

## Product and route capabilities

Core model: a curated, static library of trails with reviews, photos, difficulty ratings, and filters. Navigation, offline maps, and wrong-turn alerts layer on top. Multi-activity (hiking, running, biking) but hike-first in content and brand. [verified] (TIME article above; Permira portfolio page, https://www.permira.com/portfolio/our-portfolio/alltrails, accessed 2026-07-30)

Peak tier route features (launched May 12, 2025):

- Custom Routes: build a route from scratch (tap points or draw a line) or modify an existing AllTrails route, on app or web, downloadable offline. [verified] (Popular Science, "The new AllTrails Peak subscription offers customizable routes, real-time trail conditions, and more", https://www.popsci.com/gear/alltrails-peak-subscription-hiking-app/, accessed 2026-07-30)
- AI smart routing accepts exactly four adjustment commands on an existing route: reverse direction, make it shorter, reduce elevation gain, take the scenic route (which may increase length to hit scenic stops). [verified] (Popular Science above; AllTrails support, "Introducing Peak", https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/36898595090836-Introducing-Peak, accessed 2026-07-30; Field Mag, "Is AllTrails Peak Worth It? Here's My Hands-on Review", https://www.fieldmag.com/articles/alltrails-peak-hiking-app-review, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Routing snaps only to marked trails, paths, and roads; off-track routing is not possible. A hands-on reviewer confirmed taps on unmarked terrain do nothing. [verified] (We Are Explorers, "Is AllTrails Peak Worth It?", https://weareexplorers.co/is-alltrails-peak-worth-it/, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Trail Conditions: forecast along the trail (temperature, precipitation, snow depth, air quality, mosquito activity), built from 15 weather factors and 10 million plus data points, sourced from Meteomatics and Tomorrow.io. This is planning information display, not a routing constraint. Ground conditions and surface type along the trail were announced as a future update. [verified] (AllTrails support, "Trail Conditions", https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/36933535617300, accessed 2026-07-30; AllTrails press, "AllTrails expands membership offering with AllTrails Peak", https://www.alltrails.com/press/alltrails-expands-membership-offering-with-alltrails-peak, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Community heatmaps: popularity overlay, Peak only. [verified] (Field Mag above)

Constraint parameters accepted for route generation, precisely: start point and waypoints (manual), plus the four AI adjustments (reverse, shorter, less elevation gain, more scenic). It does not accept target distance, elevation targets, weather, street crossings, safety, surface preference, or any training input as a generation parameter. There is no "generate me a route from here" capability at all: a human must first create or pick a route for the AI to adjust. [verified for what exists via sources above; inferred for the absences: no AllTrails documentation or review describes any further parameters]

Urban running suitability: custom routes can technically follow roads, but the library, reviews, conditions data, and brand are trail and park centric, and the AI adjustments operate on trail routes. No running-specific features (pace, workouts, turn-by-turn voice cues tuned for runners) were found. [inferred from the road-snapping confirmation in We Are Explorers above plus absence of urban running features in all coverage reviewed]

## Pricing (2026)

- Base: free (trail discovery, basic weather). [verified] (CNET, "I've Been Hiking With AllTrails for 10 Years...", https://www.cnet.com/health/fitness/how-to-hike-using-alltrails-my-go-to-outdoor-app/, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Plus: $35.99 per year (offline maps, wrong-turn alerts, most Trail Conditions). [verified] (CNET above; Field Mag above, which rounds to $36)
- Peak: $79.99 per year, roughly $80 (everything in Plus, plus Custom Routes with AI smart routing, full Trail Conditions, community heatmaps, Outdoor Lens AI identification, bulk offline area downloads). 7-day free trial at launch. [verified] (CNET, Popular Science, Field Mag above)

## Positioning

"Find your trail, plan your trip" (CEO Liz Hamren): the definitive outdoors discovery companion for recreational hikers, backpackers, and outdoor enthusiasts of all skill levels. Not positioned as a training or fitness tool; a running-industry analysis notes AllTrails' community engagement is low compared to Strava and its audience is recreational outdoor explorers rather than athletes. [verified] (TIME article above; Running on Insights, "AllTrails is Peak and yet does it have a community engagement", https://runningoninsights.substack.com/p/alltrails-is-peak-and-yet-does-it, accessed 2026-07-30)

## Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths:

- Massive content moat: 500,000 plus verified trails with 15 years of reviews, photos, and condition reports. [verified] (TIME article above)
- Huge, growing, mainstream user base and strong brand trust (2023 iPhone App of the Year, TIME100 2026 company). [verified] (TIME article above; Running on Insights above)
- Profitable, well capitalized, and shipping fast in 2025 to 2026 (Peak, AI features, satellite, ChatGPT integration). [verified] (TechCrunch and TIME above)
- Trail Conditions is the most weather-aware planning feature of any competitor profiled. [verified] (AllTrails support above)

Weaknesses:

- [verified] AI routing is adjustment-only, on-trail-network-only, and cannot generate a route from constraints; reviewers found the scenic option unpredictably inflates distance and elevation.
- [inferred] No training context: AllTrails knows nothing about a user's workout plan, pace, or fitness, so it cannot answer "what should I run today". Reasoning: no training features exist anywhere in the product line.
- [inferred] Urban runner blindspot: value concentrates in parks and trails; a city runner starting from their front door gets little from the library or conditions data. Reasoning: content model is trail-page based, and coverage of Peak is entirely hike framed.
- [inferred] Peak upsell friction: reviewers questioned whether Peak justifies $44 per year over Plus, and long-time Plus members resented custom routes being paywalled a tier higher. Reasoning: explicit conclusions in We Are Explorers and GearJunkie coverage.

## Review sentiment and common complaints

- Positive: mainstream reviewers rate Peak's custom routes "extremely easy (dare I say fun?) to use" (Field Mag) and useful for getting the most out of trails (Popular Science). [verified] (both cited above)
- "As it turns out, you can only customise and link up routes along marked trails, paths, and roads": disappointment from users expecting off-track flexibility, and a verdict that Plus remains the better value. [verified] (We Are Explorers above)
- GearJunkie notes the AI "can't help you find less elevation out of Zion's Kolob Canyon": the tool is bounded by local trail density. [verified] (GearJunkie, "A Bigger, Better AllTrails Debuts Peak Tier and 6 New Features", https://gearjunkie.com/technology/alltrails-debuts-peak-tier-6-new-features, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Safety criticism: search and rescue professionals and Reddit commenters warn the AI features breed "digital overconfidence" in inexperienced hikers, and that the app sometimes mislabels terrain. [verified that this criticism was published; the underlying safety claim itself is opinion] (Vice, "I'm an Avid Hiker and AllTrails User. Are the New AI Features Any Good?", https://www.vice.com/en/article/avid-hiker-and-alltrails-new-ai-features/, accessed 2026-07-30, plus Reddit reaction summarized in search results, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Community engagement on social features is low relative to Strava. [verified] (Running on Insights above)

## Threat assessment for Waypoint

Medium threat today, high optionality. AllTrails owns the "AI route generation" narrative in consumer minds, has 95 million users, is profitable, and demonstrated in 2025 that it can ship AI routing features quickly, so a pivot of Custom Routes from adjustment-only to constraint-based generation is well within its technical reach. However, its center of gravity (trails, parks, recreational hikers) is far from Waypoint's committed amateur urban runner, it has no training or coaching layer, and its AI accepts no distance, weather, safety, or workout constraints. The most credible threat vector is AllTrails extending Trail Conditions plus generation into a "plan my run" feature for its existing runners on trails, which would still leave doorstep urban running uncontested. Waypoint should not compete on trail content and should define the category around training-adaptive, city-capable generation before AllTrails' inevitable feature creep reaches it.
