# Competitor Profile: Joggo

Version-Timestamp: 2026-07-30 14:30:00 UTC-4

Joggo is a beginner and weight-loss oriented running app from Lithuanian digital wellness group Kilo Health, combining quiz-generated running plans, meal plans, and a GPS tracker behind a quiz-funnel subscription. Its "coaching" is template-based with bi-weekly adjustments rather than real adaptive AI, and its reputation is dominated by billing and refund complaints on Trustpilot. It targets a different user (aspiring runners losing weight) than Waypoint's committed amateur, and it has no route intelligence beyond basic GPS tracking.

## 1. Company snapshot

- Ownership: Joggo is a product of Kilo Health (payments processed by Kilo grupe, UAB, company code 303157579, Vilnius, Lithuania), a venture studio operating a portfolio of direct-to-consumer wellness apps. - Founded/launched: the iOS app (id 1558434982) appeared around 2021. No standalone founding team is publicized; it is studio-built. [inferred from App Store id era and Kilo Health model]
- Funding: not separately funded; resourced inside Kilo Health. [inferred]
- Team size: not disclosed; shared Kilo Health resources. [inferred]
- Users, subscribers, revenue: not disclosed. Scale signals: 5.3K US App Store ratings, 991 Trustpilot reviews, roughly 9K total store ratings per third-party aggregator. [verified] (Sources: Apple App Store, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/joggo-run-tracker-coach/id1558434982, accessed 2026-07-30; Trustpilot, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/joggo.run, accessed 2026-07-30; MWM app profile, https://mwm.ai/apps/joggo-run-tracker-coach/1558434982, accessed 2026-07-30)

## 2. Product

- Onboarding quiz plus a short assessment run generates a personalized running program; plans cover walking starts, couch to 5K, up to half marathon, with treadmill mode. [verified] (Source: Google Play, "Joggo - Run Tracker & Coach", https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.innodiets.joggo, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Adaptivity: "Complete an assessment run every two weeks and unlock new running levels... We'll analyze your results and feedback and adjust your running program accordingly." Bi-weekly, assessment-gated adjustment of template plans built by trainers. No per-run adaptation, no physiological modeling, no AI claims beyond marketing. This is the shallowest coaching engine in the set. [verified for mechanism; inferred for depth ranking] (Source: Joggo, "Your Personal Running App", https://joggo.run/en-US/running-app, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Bundled extras: certified-nutritionist meal plans (weight-loss oriented), equipment-free strength workouts (some as paid add-ons), audio guidance, educational articles, streak medals. [verified] (Same sources)
- Platforms: iOS and Android. Wearables: Apple Watch only (run tracking, heart rate zone guidance, Apple Health sync). No Garmin, Coros, Polar, or Strava integration; testers note double-recording is required for other ecosystems. [verified] (Source: Garage Gym Reviews, "Expert-Tested: Joggo Review (2026)", https://www.garagegymreviews.com/joggo-review, accessed 2026-07-30)

## 3. Route and location capabilities (Waypoint wedge check)

- GPS run tracking only: distance, speed, activity history, and an outdoor map view of the run in progress. No route generation, no route planning, no route discovery, no navigation, no weather or terrain awareness. [verified] (Sources: Google Play listing and MWM profile above)
- Known GPS weaknesses: the app warns location services may fail when the app is backgrounded or the phone is locked; testers measured accuracy discrepancies versus Garmin. Its route-adjacent capability is below the baseline of free trackers. [verified] (Garage Gym Reviews above)
- Net: zero overlap with Waypoint's wedge, and the tracker quality bar it sets is low. [verified]

## 4. Pricing (checked 2026-07-30)

- Quiz-funnel subscription, no free trial. Reference pricing from expert review: $33 per month monthly, $46 per 3 months, $66 per 6 months, with heavy promo discounting in the web funnel. [verified] (Garage Gym Reviews above)
- App Store in-app purchase list shows wide variation: 1-month $49.00, 3-month $46.99 to $69.99, 6-month $64.99 to $100.00, 12-month $93.99 to $99.99, plus paid workout add-ons ($19.99). Pricing is inconsistent by channel and promo cohort, a hallmark of the Kilo funnel model. [verified] (Source: Apple App Store AU listing, https://apps.apple.com/au/app/joggo-run-tracker-coach/id1558434982, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Auto-renewal by default; refund policy requires contacting support within 14 days with "visual proof that the product was faulty." Effectively a no-refund posture. [verified] (Source: Joggo Help Center, "Joggo refund policy", https://help.joggo.run/hc/en-us/articles/4406966856465-Joggo-refund-policy, accessed 2026-07-30)

## 5. Positioning and marketing

- Self-description: "your personal running coach, nutritionist, and support group, right in your pocket." Weight loss is a primary promise alongside running progress. [verified] (Joggo running app page above)
- Target segment: beginners, aspiring runners, and people using running for weight loss; explicitly framed by Kilo Health as suited to "individuals dealing with excess weight." Minimal overlap with committed amateurs. [verified] (Kilo Health page above)
- Channels: performance marketing quiz funnels (web checkout outside app stores), app store listings, refer-a-friend and rewards mechanics. Also sold B2B through Kilo Health for Business wellness bundles. [verified for funnel and B2B; inferred for paid acquisition weight]

## 6. Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths:
- Low barrier for true beginners: simple plans, audio guidance, meal plans in one bundle. [verified]
- Kilo Health's funnel machine can buy users at scale across geographies. [inferred from parent company model]
- Treadmill mode and equipment-free strength content fit its homebody audience. [verified]

Weaknesses:
- Trust deficit: persistent billing, cancellation, and refund complaints dominate Trustpilot; the refund policy is adversarial. [verified, below]
- Shallow adaptivity (bi-weekly template adjustment) and no wearable ecosystem beyond Apple Watch. [verified]
- GPS reliability problems in the app's core tracking function. [verified]
- Price per value is poor versus Runna or Coopah at comparable monthly cost. [inferred: comparable or higher monthly price with far less coaching depth]

## 7. Review sentiment

- Ratings: 4.4 on the US App Store (5.3K ratings, checked 2026-07-30); Google Play around 3.6 per Garage Gym Reviews; Trustpilot average around 4 across 991 reviews but with a heavy tail of 1-star billing complaints. [verified] (Sources: Apple App Store, Garage Gym Reviews, Trustpilot, all accessed 2026-07-30)
- Praise themes: approachable for beginners, warm-ups and cool-downs included, "The audio encouragement is also quite nice. You can play your own music in [the background]," no technical issues for some users. Observed at: Trustpilot. [verified]
- Complaint theme 1, subscription and refund traps (dominant): "hooks you on a subscription, doesn't give you any communication when renewal is coming up, and then refuses to refund you the annual fee... even when you contact them within 24 hours of payment"; "I have a written confirmation via email that I cancelled the service, yet they charged me again"; a promised refund only materialized after the user threatened a bank dispute. Observed at: Trustpilot (multiple reviews), echoed by cancellation-help site Postclic. [verified] (Sources: Trustpilot reviews, https://www.trustpilot.com/review/joggo.run, accessed 2026-07-30; Postclic, "How to Cancel Joggo", https://www.postclic.com/en-ph/letters/fitness/cancel-joggo/, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Complaint theme 2, deceptive challenge marketing: a "complete the challenge, get your money back" offer that fine-print converts into a recurring subscription at a higher price. Observed at: Trustpilot. [verified]
- Complaint theme 3, tracking and sync failures: watch and phone sessions do not sync ("if you start on your watch [it's] not your scheduled workout, just a [free] run"); GPS requires the app open in the foreground. Observed at: AU App Store reviews, Garage Gym Reviews testing. [verified]
- Complaint theme 4, support unresponsiveness: meal plan lost after a phone change, support never followed up, refund window cited as 24 hours. Observed at: Trustpilot (UK). [verified]
- Reddit presence in r/running is negligible for Joggo; serious runner communities do not discuss it as a training option. [verified as observation, inferred significance: it competes in the diet-app aisle, not the running aisle]

## 8. Threat assessment for Waypoint

Low. Joggo targets beginner weight-loss users through paid quiz funnels, a different buyer, different job-to-be-done, and different channel than Waypoint's committed amateur runner. It has no adaptive coaching depth, no wearable ecosystem, no route intelligence, and a trust problem that would poison any move upmarket. Two indirect relevances: first, Kilo Health has the capital and funnel expertise to clone a concept quickly if adaptive route generation proves demand, so Waypoint should expect fast followers from studio players; second, Joggo's Trustpilot record is a live demonstration of how subscription dark patterns destroy category trust, reinforcing that Waypoint's freemium mechanics must be conspicuously clean (clear renewal notices, easy cancellation, honest trials).
