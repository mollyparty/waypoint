# Competitor Profile: Coopah

Version-Timestamp: 2026-07-30 14:30:00 UTC-4

Coopah is a London-based run coaching app that pairs adaptive training plans with 24/7 access to real human coaches, backed by London Marathon Events and positioned as the friendlier, cheaper alternative to Runna. Its differentiators are the human coaching layer, a PB money-back guarantee, and official training partner status for the TCS London Marathon. It has no route or location intelligence, modest funding, and a plan engine reviewers describe as more conservative and more rigid than Runna's.

## 2. Product

- Personalized, adaptive training plans built by elite coaches and adjusted to fitness and schedule; strength and conditioning sessions included in every plan. [verified] (Source: Apple App Store, "Coopah: The Run Coaching App", https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coopah-the-run-coaching-app/id1587165314, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Coaching model: hybrid. "24/7 Expert Coaching combines instant AI answers with personal support from real human coaches" who can see the user's training data. The human layer is the core differentiator versus Runna and pure-algorithm apps. [verified] (App Store listing above; Women's Running, "Train smarter with personalised coaching from Coopah", https://www.womensrunning.co.uk/gear/coopah-running-app-personalised-coaching/, 2026-02-17, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Adaptivity triggers: completed runs and training data, schedule changes (sessions can be rescheduled), goal race setup with recent race times. Race Day Score gives a 0 to 100% readiness assessment. Weekly Coach's Report reviews mileage and session quality. [verified] (App Store listing above)
- Distances: 5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon, plus a "Just Run" non-race mode. Conflict on ultras: All About Ultras (2025) lists ultramarathon plans; a January 2026 comparison video states Coopah stops at marathon while Runna offers ultras. Weighting: the more recent source wins; treated as marathon-capped with possible legacy ultra options. [verified conflict, presented] (Sources: All About Ultras, "Coopah: An Affordable and Accessible Running App for Beginners", https://allaboutultras.com/coopah-an-affordable-and-accessible-running-app-for-beginners/, accessed 2026-07-30; YouTube, "RUNNA vs COOPAH", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J0_75Tt2okM, accessed 2026-07-30)
- PB Guarantee: complete the plan, miss the goal, get a refund. [verified] (Women's Running above)
- Platforms: iOS and Android. Wearables: Apple Watch (including workout sync to watch), Garmin, Polar, Coros. [verified] (Source: Tom's Guide, "Coopah review", https://www.tomsguide.com/wellness/fitness/im-using-the-coopah-running-app-to-train-for-a-marathon-and-its-better-than-runna-for-beginners, 2025-01-23, accessed 2026-07-30)

## 6. Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths:
- Human coaching access at an app price point; strongest support reputation in the set (automated check-ins after missed training, caring coach responses). [verified via reviews below]
- London Marathon partnership is a durable, high-intent acquisition channel. [verified]
- PB Guarantee is a distinctive trust lever. [verified]
- Cheaper than Runna with comparable plan structure. [verified]

Weaknesses:
- Plans described as more conservative and more rigid than Runna's (minimum 8-week plans, cannot change event date, limited handling of cross-training). [verified via reviews below]
- Some users suspect the "coaches" in chat are partly bots, which undercuts the core differentiator if perception spreads. [verified as user perception, below]
- No route, weather, or location intelligence; also no ultra support. [verified]
- Small funding base against a Strava-owned market leader. [inferred]

## 8. Threat assessment for Waypoint

Medium. Coopah competes directly for Waypoint's committed amateur target (race-oriented, 3 to 5 runs per week) with a genuinely differentiated human coaching layer and a privileged London Marathon acquisition channel, and its sub-Runna pricing brackets the freemium price band Waypoint is considering. However, it has zero route or location capability, no weather adaptation, a conservative and rigid plan engine, and only seed-scale resources, so it cannot quickly follow Waypoint into constraint-based route generation. The real lesson is on positioning: Coopah wins hearts through support and reassurance, which suggests Waypoint's coaching layer needs warmth, not just intelligence. Threat rises if Coopah's human coaches start recommending where to run, a low-tech version of Waypoint's answer.
