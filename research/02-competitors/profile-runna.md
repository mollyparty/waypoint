# Competitor Profile: Runna

Version-Timestamp: 2026-07-30 14:30:00 UTC-4

Runna is the category leader in AI-assisted running training plans, acquired by Strava in April 2025 and operated as a standalone business. It sells adaptive, coach-designed training plans (5K to ultramarathon) with strong wearable integrations, and in mid-2026 shipped weather-adaptive coaching (Adapt for Heat). It has no route generation of its own, but its owner Strava controls the largest community route dataset in the industry, which makes Runna the most dangerous competitor to Waypoint's route-intelligence wedge.

## 1. Company snapshot

- Founded: 2021 (company formation reported as 2020 by Tracxn; official app launch March 2022) by Dom Maskell and Ben Parker, London, UK. [verified] (Source: Strava Press Room, "Strava to Acquire Runna, A Leading Running Training App", https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-to-acquire-runna-a-leading-running-training-app, 2025-04-17, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Ownership: acquired by Strava, announced 2025-04-17. Strava states Runna continues as a standalone business "for the foreseeable future" and that it is investing in growing the Runna team. [verified] (Source: Strava Help Center, "Strava is Acquiring Runna", https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401589-strava-is-acquiring-runna, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Pre-acquisition funding: approximately $10.3M across 5 rounds; £485K crowdfunding (2021 to 2022), £2.25M seed led by Eka Ventures, £5M Series A led by JamJar Investments (December 2023). ## 2. Product

- Personalized training plans for 5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon, and ultra, built by an in-house coaching team (including elite athletes) and adapted algorithmically week to week based on completed runs, estimated fitness, and user feedback. [verified] (Source: Strava Help Center article above; Runna Support, "App Features", https://support.runna.com/en/collections/16740555-app-features, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Coaching engine is algorithmic plan adaptation layered on coach-designed templates, marketed as AI. No public evidence of LLM-generated plans. [inferred: marketing and support docs describe rules and adaptation triggers, not generative AI]
- Adaptivity triggers: completed run performance, "Not Feeling 100%" feedback on some plans, Runna Score fitness estimates, and since July 2026 local heat and humidity (Adapt for Heat automatically slows target paces and shows a color-coded hourly heat forecast). [verified] (Source: Engadget, "Runna's new coaching feature will adjust your workout based on local heat and humidity", https://www.engadget.com/2226300/runnas-new-coaching-feature-will-adjust-your-workout-based-on-local-heat-and-humidity/, 2026-07-29, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Pipeline announced for August 2026: Upcoming Plans (stack multiple plans up to a year ahead) and revamped Post-Race Recovery Plans. [verified] (Source: endurance.biz, "Runna updates app to support year-round training", https://endurance.biz/2026/industry-news/runna-updates-app-to-support-year-round-training/, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Platforms: iOS and Android. Wearables: Apple Watch, Garmin, Coros supported (Fitbit also cited in third-party profiles); Strava sync. [verified] (Sources: The Running Genie, "Runna After the Strava Acquisition", https://therunninggenie.com/blog/is-runna-still-worth-it-after-strava-acquisition, accessed 2026-07-30)

## 3. Route and location capabilities (Waypoint wedge check)

- Runna has NO native route generation, planning, or discovery. Its own comparison page states: "Runna doesn't currently offer route planning or community-based discovery tools." [verified] (Source: Runna, "Runna vs Strava", https://www.runna.com/runna-vs-strava, accessed 2026-07-30)
- It can FOLLOW routes: users add public routes from Strava into the Runna app or Apple Watch for turn guidance during a workout. This is import and follow, not generation. [verified] (Source: Runna Support, "How to Follow a Route in the Runna App", https://support.runna.com/en/collections/16740555-app-features, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Adapt for Heat (July 2026) is location-aware weather intelligence applied to pacing, not to route choice. It answers "how fast," not "where." [verified] (Engadget source above)
- The strategic risk sits with the owner: Strava Premium has a route builder with heatmaps, elevation, and surface type, plus community route discovery, and the Strava+Runna bundle marketing already promises "personalized routes linked to your training plan." A deep integration of Strava routes into Runna workouts would replicate a large part of Waypoint's wedge. [verified for current Strava capabilities and bundle marketing; inferred for future integration] (Source: Strava Press Room, "Strava + Runna Launch Combined Subscription Bundle", https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-runna-launch-combined-subscription-bundle, 2025-07-02, accessed 2026-07-30)
- As of 2026-07-30 there is no evidence of on-demand, constraint-based route generation (distance, elevation, crossings, safety, surface) in either Runna or Strava. [verified: absence in current feature documentation]

## 4. Pricing (checked 2026-07-30)

- Free trial: 1 week standard, commonly extended to 2 weeks with promo codes. No meaningful free tier; the app is subscription gated. [verified for trial; inferred for free tier scope]

## 5. Positioning and marketing

- Self-description: "#1 rated personalised running coaching app," democratizing elite coaching. Positions against human coaches on price and against generic plans on personalization. [verified] (Runna blog above)
- Target segment: committed recreational racers, first-time race finishers through experienced club runners. Overlaps directly with Waypoint's committed amateur hypothesis. [inferred from plan lineup and race partnerships]
- Channels: race partnerships (Great North Run, LA Marathon), elite athlete ambassadors (Beth Potter, Steph Davis), App Store featuring, running influencer content, and now Strava's 150M+ registered user funnel and bundle. [verified] (Runna blog, Strava press releases above)

## 6. Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths:
- Distribution moat via Strava ownership and bundle; largest brand awareness in the category. [verified]
- Polished UX, strong watch execution, credible coaching staff. [verified via ratings and reviews]
- Fast feature velocity in 2026 (heat adaptation, multi-plan scheduling). [verified]

Weaknesses:
- Persistent public discourse that plans push too hard and contribute to injuries; default progressions are aggressive for people who overstate fitness. [inferred: anecdote-driven, no comparative study exists, but the perception itself is a market fact] (Source: the5krunner, "Runna: Is Your AI Marathon Training Plan Injuring You?", https://the5krunner.com/2026/02/21/runna-ai-marathon-training-injury/, 2026-02-21, accessed 2026-07-30)
- No route intelligence in-product; depends on Strava for anything location-based. [verified]
- Post-acquisition ambiguity: Strava ships its own Athlete Intelligence AI, raising questions about long-run investment split. [inferred] (Source: The Running Genie article above)
- Reported billing and support friction around the Strava bundle, and watch sync reliability complaints. [verified via App Store reviews, below]

## 7. Review sentiment

- App Store rating: 4.8 (Canadian storefront, checked 2026-07-30). [verified] (Source: Apple App Store, "Runna: Running Plans & Coach - Ratings & Reviews", https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/runna-running-plans-coach/id1594204443, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Praise themes: personal bests and first race finishes, plan structure and clarity, watch integration, physio and strength content. [verified] (the5krunner review roundup above)
- Complaint theme 1, aggressive pacing and injury fear: widespread on Reddit (r/running), TikTok, and Threads through early 2026; users report stress fractures and shin splints they attribute to the plans. Direct quote from a user comment: "the training paces were always borderline too fast for me... even a easy day was a grind" (user who switched to Coopah). Observed at: the5krunner comments, hereandthere.club analysis of the Reddit and TikTok discourse. [verified as sentiment pattern] (Sources: the5krunner above; Here and There Club, "Is Runna Actually Getting Runners Injured?", https://www.hereandthere.club/p/is-runna-actually-getting-runners-injured, accessed 2026-07-30; Lifehacker AU, "How to Avoid Injury While Using Runna Training Programs", https://au.lifehacker.com/fitness/117888/feature/how-to-avoid-injury-while-using-runna-training-programs, accessed 2026-07-30)
- Complaint theme 2, billing and support: App Store reviewer paraphrase: charged for both Strava and Runna when trying to link bundle accounts, 21 days of support silence, refund refused. Observed at: Apple App Store reviews (CA storefront). [verified]
- Complaint theme 3, sync reliability: App Store reviewer reports Apple Watch runs tracked live but failing to appear in Runna or Strava history "about 25% of the time." Observed at: Apple App Store reviews. [verified]
- Complaint theme 4, insufficient easy running: reviewers and coaches note too much moderate-intensity work for newer runners. Observed at: Lifehacker AU analysis, Reddit discourse. [verified as pattern]

## 8. Threat assessment for Waypoint

High. Runna owns the exact target user (committed amateur racing occasionally), has the strongest brand in AI running coaching, and now sits inside Strava, which owns the community route graph, heatmaps, and surface data that Waypoint would need to out-execute. If Strava wires its route discovery into Runna's daily workouts, it delivers a good-enough version of "where should I run today" with zero acquisition cost. The offsetting facts: neither app generates constraint-aware routes on demand today, Runna's route story is import-and-follow only, and the loud injury discourse plus bundle billing friction create a trust opening. Waypoint should treat Runna as the incumbent to differentiate against on route generation depth (safety, crossings, surface, weather) rather than on coaching quality.
