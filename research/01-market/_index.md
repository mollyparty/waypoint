## Headline findings

- The route-generation whitespace is real: training apps prescribe workouts without location intelligence, routing apps plan static routes without training context; nobody generates the run itself, and the enabling stack (OSM routing, free elevation and weather, on-device AI) is nearly zero marginal cost.
- H5 (venture scale) verdict: UNCERTAIN. The wedge alone benchmarks to roughly $5M to $30M ARR by year 5 (anchored on Runna's ~90k payers at acquisition); venture scale requires an expansion story.
- 2025 consolidation reshaped the map: Strava bought Runna, Bending Spoons bought Komoot; platform giants (Garmin Connect+, Apple Workout Buddy) are commoditizing generic AI coaching.
- Garmin has paused new Connect Developer Program applications: a launch risk for wearable integration beyond HealthKit.
- Top compliance surfaces: precise location data (routes reveal home addresses), GDPR Article 9 health data, and EU AI Act Article 50 transparency (applies 2026-08-02). A 15-item MVP compliance checklist is in `regulatory-compliance.md`.
