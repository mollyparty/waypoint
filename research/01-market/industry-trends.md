# Industry Trends: AI Running Coaching and Adaptive Route Generation

Version-Timestamp: 2026-07-30 14:05:00 UTC-4

Executive summary: three trends matter most for Waypoint. First, the platform squeeze: wearable makers are simultaneously shipping their own AI coaching (Garmin Connect+, Apple Workout Buddy) and tightening third-party data access (Garmin has paused all new Connect Developer Program applications with no reopening date), which makes Waypoint's iOS-first, HealthKit-centered architecture a strategic necessity rather than a preference. Second, the hero feature is still unowned: incumbents have shipped AI commentary (Strava Athlete Intelligence), AI chat (WHOOP Coach), and adaptive plans (Runna), but no major player answers "where should I run, right now, from here, for me," and the enabling stack (OpenStreetMap plus open-source routers with round-trip generation, free WeatherKit, free on-device 3B parameter LLM via Apple's Foundation Models framework) has never been cheaper. Third, the demand wave is real and young: running participation grew by more than 110 million people globally between 2022 and 2025, Gen Z is driving a run club and racing boom, and Health and Fitness is the one app category where annual subscriptions still dominate revenue, supporting a premium subscription model if, and only if, retention holds.

## 1. Wearables: penetration, data access, and first-party coaching

### 1.1 The installed base keeps growing, led by Apple and premium devices

What is happening:

- IDC counted 47.05 million wrist-worn devices shipped in Q1 2026, up 2.2 percent year over year. Smartwatches were 37.03 million of those, up 4.8 percent, while basic fitness bands fell 6.1 percent. [verified] (Source: IDC data via Wedoany, "IDC: Global Wrist-Worn Device Shipments Reach 47.05 Million Units in Q1 2026," https://en.wedoany.com/shortnews/244114.html, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Apple shipped 8 million wrist-worn units in Q1 2026 (up 13.2 percent year over year); Garmin shipped 2.4 million (up 12.7 percent, 5.1 percent share). High-end models above 300 dollars are growing fastest, driven by health and AI feature upgrades. [verified] (Source: same IDC via Wedoany article; TechTimes, "Smartwatch Market Hits 37M Units in Q1," https://www.techtimes.com/articles/318223/20260611/smartwatch-market-hits-37m-units-q1-fitness-wristbands-shrink-galaxy-watch-9-nears.htm, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Counterpoint Research puts Apple at 23 percent of Q1 2026 smartwatch units, up from 20 percent a year earlier on 21 percent volume growth, with North America contributing over half of Apple's shipments. Counterpoint forecasts roughly 3 percent CAGR for wearables through 2030. [verified] (Source: PhoneArena, "Apple's smartwatch market share keeps growing," https://www.phonearena.com/news/q1-2026-smartwatch-market-report-apple-share-growing-samsung-struggles-deepening_id181208, accessed 2026-07-30; GSMArena, "Smartwatch shipments grew steadily in Q1 2026," https://www.gsmarena.com/smartwatch_shipments_grew_steadily_in_q1_2026_with_apple_still_leading_the_pack-news-73343.php, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Source conflict: IDC's tracker shows Apple leading at 21.5 percent unit share and forecasts full-year 2026 smartwatch volumes of 159.7 million units, down 2.8 percent, citing memory supply constraints; Counterpoint reports Q1 growth and a modestly positive outlook. I weight IDC for the full-year forecast because it explicitly models the memory supply constraint, and I treat Counterpoint's premiumization signal as the more durable strategic takeaway. [verified, conflict noted] (Source: IDC, "Wearable Devices Market Insights," https://www.idc.com/promo/wearablevendor/, accessed 2026-07-30.)

Why it matters to Waypoint: the committed amateur runner increasingly owns a premium watch, so Waypoint can assume rich sensor data (heart rate, pace, elevation, training load) exists for its target user without building hardware.

Classification: tailwind.

### 1.2 Third-party data access is bifurcating: HealthKit open, Garmin closing

What is happening:

- HealthKit remains fully open to third-party apps on iOS: workouts, heart rate, and related samples are readable with user consent. Since iOS 18 and watchOS 11, workout effort scores (workoutEffortScore and estimatedWorkoutEffortScore, a 0 to 10 RPE scale feeding Apple's Training Load feature) are readable and writable by third-party apps, though developers complain documentation is thin. [verified] (Source: Sasquatch Studio, "Reading from, and Saving Workout Effort to, HealthKit," https://sasq.ca/blog/2025/4/28/reading-writing-workout-effort-scores, accessed 2026-07-30; Apple Developer Forums, "Missing manual - Training Load, workoutEffortScore," https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/765438, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- The Garmin Connect Developer Program (Health, Activity, Training, Courses APIs over OAuth 2.0) has no base license fee but is business use only, and some metrics require license fees or minimum device order quantities. [verified] (Source: Garmin Developers, "Program FAQ, Garmin Connect Developer Program," https://developer.garmin.com/gc-developer-program/program-faq/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Garmin has paused all new developer program applications: the application form was removed in 2026, Garmin confirmed to at least one applicant that there is no projected reopening date and no notification list, and small coaching app developers report rejections and months of silence. [verified] (Source: Garmin Forums, "Garmin Connect Developer Program - Access Request Rejected Without Notification," https://forums.garmin.com/developer/connect-iq/f/discussion/434542/, accessed 2026-07-30; Garmin Forums, "Apply for Connect Developer Program - Access Request Form down?," https://forums.garmin.com/developer/connect-iq/f/discussion/434761/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Garmin also deployed Cloudflare TLS fingerprinting that blocks unofficial API clients and scrapers, closing the informal workaround path. Affected developers are falling back to third-party aggregators such as Terra. [verified] (Source: Garmin Forums, "TLS fingerprinting blocks third-party clients," https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-web/433892/, accessed 2026-07-30.)

Why it matters to Waypoint: Waypoint's iOS-first plan means HealthKit covers the Apple Watch majority of its target market, but direct Garmin integration (important for the most serious runners) may be blocked at launch, forcing reliance on user-initiated FIT file export, Strava as an intermediary, or a paid aggregator.

Classification: both. Tailwind on Apple, headwind on Garmin.

### 1.3 Watch makers are building their own coaching

What is happening:

- Garmin launched Connect+ on March 27, 2025 at 6.99 dollars per month or 69.99 dollars per year, with AI-powered "Active Intelligence" insights (in beta at launch) and extra Garmin Coach guidance, while keeping existing features free. [verified] (Source: Garmin, "Elevate your health and fitness goals with Garmin Connect+," https://www.garmin.com/en-US/newsroom/press-release/wearables-health/elevate-your-health-and-fitness-goals-with-garmin-connect/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Reception among long-time Garmin users was hostile (subscription fatigue on top of premium hardware prices), and reviewers judged the AI insights thin; still, Garmin told investors in its Q2 2025 earnings call that subscription revenue is growing across all segments. [verified] (Source: The Verge, "Garmin adds AI and a subscription tier to its app," https://www.theverge.com/news/636211/garmin-connect-plus-subscription-wearables, accessed 2026-07-30; DC Rainmaker, "Garmin Connect+ Paid Subscription: Hands-on Thoughts & Analysis," https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2025/03/garmin-connect-plus-subscription-walkthrough.html, accessed 2026-07-30; Gadgets and Wearables, "Garmin sees rising traction for Connect Plus," https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2025/08/01/garmin-connect-plus-subscriptions/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Apple's shipped equivalent is Workout Buddy in watchOS 26 and iOS 26 (announced June 2025): an Apple Intelligence generative voice, built from Fitness+ trainer voice data, that delivers motivational commentary (pep talk at the start, splits and milestones during, summary at the end) for runs, walks, cycling, HIIT, and strength sessions. It requires an Apple Intelligence capable iPhone (15 Pro or newer) nearby and Bluetooth headphones, English only at launch. [verified] (Source: Apple Newsroom, "watchOS 26 delivers more personalized ways to stay active and connected," June 2025, https://www.apple.com/ml/newsroom/2025/06/watchos-26-delivers-more-personalized-ways-to-stay-active-and-connected/, accessed 2026-07-30; Engadget, "How to use Workout Buddy with Apple Watch and iOS 26," https://www.engadget.com/wearables/how-to-use-workout-buddy-with-apple-watch-and-ios-26-130000922.html, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Important scoping point: Workout Buddy is motivation and commentary, not a training plan generator, not adaptive coaching, and not route generation. Through the watchOS 26 era Apple has shipped no prescriptive AI coach. [verified against Apple's own feature description above]
- The broader pattern: WHOOP shipped the first LLM coach in late 2023, Strava added AI activity summaries in 2024, Samsung added AI insights and Google added AI-generated running workouts in 2024; Garmin joined in 2025, leaving Apple the last major holdout on generative coaching until Workout Buddy. [verified] (Source: The Verge article cited above, accessed 2026-07-30.)

Why it matters to Waypoint: platform owners are normalizing AI coaching, which educates the market for Waypoint, while leaving the prescriptive layer ("what should I do, where should I go") unfilled; but if Apple extends Workout Buddy from commentary to prescription it would compete with distribution Waypoint cannot match.

Classification: both.

## 2. AI coaching adoption: attitudes, launches, trust

What is happening:

- Strava Athlete Intelligence (launched October 2024) generates narrative post-activity summaries. A peer-reviewed style analysis of 297 r/Strava threads and 5,692 comments (2,611 contributors, October 2024 to March 2025) found users did not treat the AI summaries as definitive, benchmarked them against their own training knowledge and bodily experience, and mostly found them misaligned with lived training experience. [verified] (Source: arXiv, "Who Gets to Interpret the Workout? User Tensions With AI-Generated Fitness Feedback," https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23830, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- WHOOP Coach (OpenAI-powered, launched late 2023, the first LLM coach from a major wearable) gets split reception: users credit it with useful workout comparisons, plan suggestions, and illness check-ins with memory, but others document hallucinations (in one reported case the coach discussed elevation data and then admitted inventing it) and erratic responses. Commentators note errors are costlier in health contexts because biometric grounding makes users over-trust outputs. [verified] (Source: Recombu, "Whoop's new AI fitness tools have users split," https://www.recombu.com/health/whoop-new-ai-fitness-tools-have-users-split, accessed 2026-07-30; Gear Patrol, "Are Whoop's New AI Tools the Future of Fitness, or a Cautionary Tale?," https://www.gearpatrol.com/fitness/whoop-ai-reddit-user-feedback-pros-cons/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Academic evidence on trust: a 36-participant study of a WHOOP Coach style conversational agent found trust is driven by perceived relevance and utility of responses, and collapses quickly on errors or generic advice. [verified] (Source: Chalmers University, "Give and Take: Perceptions of a Conversational Coach Agent in Fitness Trackers," https://research.chalmers.se/publication/548305/file/548305_Fulltext.pdf, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Runna, the category-leading running coach app, is deliberately not an LLM coach: its adaptation engine is coach-designed and algorithmic. Its "Adapt for Heat" feature (shipped July 29, 2026) automatically adjusts pace targets when feels-like temperature exceeds about 20 C (68 F), scaled by heat, humidity, and session duration, with Upcoming Plans and a relaunched Post-Race Recovery Plan following in August 2026. [verified] (Source: Runna, "Runna Announces New Updates... Adapt for Heat," https://www.runna.com/press/runna-announces-new-updates, accessed 2026-07-30; Garmin Rumors, "Runna Rolls Out Adapt for Heat," https://garminrumors.com/runna-rolls-out-adapt-for-heat/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Runna leadership publicly argues that purely data-driven AI coaches lack the emotional nuance to account for mental health, sleep quality, and daily stress. [verified] (Source: endurance.biz, "Runna updates app to support year-round training," https://endurance.biz/2026/industry-news/runna-updates-app-to-support-year-round-training/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Gap analysis: no incumbent AI feature is prescriptive about place. Strava summarizes, WHOOP chats, Runna plans workouts, Apple motivates; none generates the run itself as a route adapted to constraints. [inferred from the sourced landscape above]
- Trust asymmetry in Waypoint's favor: a generated route is verifiable within the hour (the runner immediately experiences whether distance, surface, elevation, and crossings matched the promise), unlike training advice whose payoff is weeks away and hard to attribute. [inferred]

Why it matters to Waypoint: consumers have been taught to expect AI in fitness apps but punish hallucination harshly; Waypoint's coaching layer must be conservative and grounded in real map, weather, and physiology data, with deterministic route math underneath the language layer.

Classification: both. Adoption tailwind, trust bar headwind.

## 3. Subscription fitness economics

What is happening:

- RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2026 (115,000+ apps, 16 billion dollars in tracked revenue) shows Health and Fitness is the only category where annual plans still dominate, at 60.6 percent of revenue; the report frames the annual commitment as part of the category's aspirational psychology. [verified] (Source: Chuvak Pavel summarizing RevenueCat and Adapty 2026 reports, "State of App Monetization 2026," https://chuvak-pavel.medium.com/state-of-app-monetization-2026-key-trends-from-revenuecat-and-adapty-8bdc23a4bb6f, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Free trials lift LTV 63.6 percent over direct purchase in Health and Fitness. Trial-to-paid conversion for the category benchmarks at 35.0 to 39.9 percent, with top quartile apps above 50 percent. [verified] (Source: same Medium summary; Mirava, "Free Trial Conversion Benchmarks for 2026," https://www.mirava.io/blog/free-trial-conversion-benchmarks-2026, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Median first renewal rates for Health and Fitness: 25 percent for annual plans, 57 percent monthly, 54 percent weekly. [verified] (Source: RevenueCat, "Average Subscription Renewal Rates by App Category (2026 Benchmarks)," https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/average-subscription-renewal-rates-by-app-category, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- The distribution is winner-take-most: the top 10 percent of apps capture 94.5 percent of subscription revenue, the median subscription app earns 492 dollars per month (down 22 percent year over year), and 57.7 percent of new apps never reach 1,000 dollars total revenue. [verified] (Source: Chuvak Pavel Medium summary cited above, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Adapty's 2026 dataset (16,000+ apps, 3 billion dollars revenue) adds that North American install-to-trial runs 14.5 percent versus 11.2 percent globally, and that the second annual renewal (about month 24) is where churn stabilizes and LTV compounds. [verified] (Source: Adapty, "In-app subscription benchmarks for Health & Fitness apps," https://adapty.io/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-benchmarks/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Reference price points in Waypoint's niche: Strava Premium about 79 dollars per year (11.99 dollars per month), Runna about 19.99 dollars per month, Garmin Connect+ 6.99 dollars per month or 69.99 dollars per year. [verified] (Source: DC Rainmaker Connect+ analysis cited above; The Running Genie, "Strava Athlete Intelligence vs Dedicated AI Running Coaches," https://therunninggenie.com/blog/strava-athlete-intelligence-vs-ai-coaches, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Bundling is here: Strava acquired Runna in April 2025, launched a combined Strava + Runna subscription on July 2, 2025 at 149.99 dollars per year (US pricing, 180+ markets, marketed as up to 60 percent off the two monthly plans), and in July 2026 pulled Runna's race database into a new Strava Events tab. [verified] (Source: Strava Press, "Strava to Acquire Runna," https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-to-acquire-runna-a-leading-running-training-app, accessed 2026-07-30; Strava Press, "Strava + Runna Launch Combined Subscription Bundle," https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-runna-launch-combined-subscription-bundle, accessed 2026-07-30; Wareable, "Strava makes its first big move since acquiring Runna," https://www.wareable.com/sport/strava-runna-combined-subscription-announcement-price, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Insurer and health plan bundling is an emerging distribution channel: Essence Healthcare (Medicare Advantage) has given qualifying members a free Oura Ring plus membership since the 2025 plan year (Ring 4 for 2026) and in June 2026 deepened this into a physician-led sleep apnea screening workflow; Discovery Vitality in South Africa funds Oura Ring 4 through a sleep reward program. [verified] (Source: Essence Healthcare press release, https://www.essencehealthcare.com/press-release/essence-healthcare-medicare-advantage-plan-members-to-receive-oura-ring-and-oura-membership-at-no-additional-cost/, accessed 2026-07-30; PR Newswire, "Essence Healthcare and OURA Deepen Partnership," June 9, 2026, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/essence-healthcare-and-ura-deepen-partnership-to-bring-sleep-health-insights-directly-into-physician-led-care-302794317.html, accessed 2026-07-30; Oura support, "Essence Healthcare," https://support.ouraring.com/hc/en-us/articles/36876485887635-Essence-Healthcare, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- No verifiable evidence of an Amazon fitness subscription bundling program material to running apps was found as of this date. [assumption A1, see below]

Why it matters to Waypoint: the category supports annual pricing and trials work, and there is a visible price umbrella between Garmin Connect+ (70 dollars per year) and Strava + Runna (150 dollars per year) for a differentiated product; but the winner-take-most revenue curve and the Strava + Runna bundle raise the bar a new entrant must clear to justify a separate subscription.

Classification: both.

## 4. Technology shifts relevant to route generation

### 4.1 Commercial mapping APIs got more complex, open-source got more attractive

What is happening:

- On March 1, 2025 Google Maps Platform replaced its 200 dollar monthly credit with per-SKU free thresholds (10,000 free monthly events for Essentials SKUs, 5,000 for Pro, 1,000 for Enterprise), moved the classic Directions API to Legacy status (no new features, cannot be newly enabled, replaced by the Routes API), and expanded volume discounts to 5 million+ monthly events. [verified] (Source: Google for Developers, "Google Maps Platform March 2025 changes," https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/march-2025, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Google later added fixed-price subscriptions: 100 dollars per month for 50,000 calls (Starter), 275 dollars for 100,000 (Essentials), 1,200 dollars for 250,000 (Pro). [verified] (Source: Google Maps Platform blog, "Announcing Google Maps Platform Subscriptions," https://mapsplatform.google.com/resources/blog/announcing-google-maps-platform-subscriptions-lower-costs-with-predictable-pricing/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- The open stack is production-ready for Waypoint's exact problem. GraphHopper's open-source routing engine ships a native round_trip algorithm: set algorithm=round_trip with ch.disable=true, control loop length with round_trip.distance, get route variety with round_trip.seed, and force an initial direction with heading. It supports foot profiles, elevation (SRTM data), and custom models that penalize or exclude road classes and areas. [verified] (Source: GraphHopper, "Calculate a route," https://docs.graphhopper.com/openapi/routing/getroute, accessed 2026-07-30; GraphHopper GitHub, "docs/web/api-doc.md," https://github.com/graphhopper/graphhopper/blob/stable/docs/web/api-doc.md, accessed 2026-07-30; GraphHopper Forum, "Circular routes," https://discuss.graphhopper.com/t/circular-routes/3313, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Valhalla is a comparable self-hostable open-source OSM router with optional elevation tile support. Both engines run on OpenStreetMap data, whose Overpass API additionally exposes the feature tags Waypoint's constraints need (crossings, footways, surface, lighting, parks). Self-hosting means zero marginal API cost per generated route. [verified for the engines and OSM tagging; the zero marginal cost framing is arithmetic] (Source: GraphHopper and Valhalla documentation via the sources above, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Weather: Apple WeatherKit includes 500,000 API calls per month with the standard Apple Developer Program membership, with paid tiers above (1 million calls for 49.99 dollars per month, scaling to 200 million), covering hyperlocal forecasts including humidity and feels-like temperature. [verified] (Source: Apple Developer, "Get Started with WeatherKit," https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/get-started/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Elevation for routing is available at no license cost via SRTM and similar open digital elevation models consumed natively by GraphHopper and Valhalla. [verified via the router documentation cited above]

### 4.2 On-device AI is now free and private on target iPhones

What is happening:

- Apple's Foundation Models framework (iOS 26) gives third-party apps direct Swift API access to the on-device 3 billion parameter Apple Intelligence model: zero inference cost, no API keys, offline capable, structured typed output via the @Generable macro, and tool calling. [verified] (Source: Apple Developer, "Meet the Foundation Models framework, WWDC25," https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/286/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Hardware floor: iPhone 15 Pro and newer (A17 Pro and up). The model is optimized for constrained tasks (summarization, extraction, classification), explicitly not world knowledge or advanced reasoning, so apps need graceful degradation on older devices. [verified] (Source: The Batch (DeepLearning.AI), "Apple Updates Its On-Device and Cloud AI Models, Introduces a New Developer API," https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/apple-updates-its-on-device-and-cloud-ai-models-introduces-a-new-developer-api, accessed 2026-07-30; HackerNoon, "A Developer's Guide to Apple's Foundation Models Framework in iOS 26," https://hackernoon.com/a-developers-guide-to-apples-foundation-models-framework-in-ios-26, accessed 2026-07-30.)

Why it matters to Waypoint: coaching narration, workout interpretation, and preference extraction can run free and private on the device Waypoint's target user already carries, while the route solver stays deterministic; this matches the trust findings in section 2 (LLM for language, algorithms for prescriptions) and keeps unit economics near zero for the AI layer.

Classification: tailwind.

### 4.3 Prior art exists in research but not as a winning product

What is happening:

- Academic prior art on constraint-based running route generation goes back a decade: ETH Zurich theses on automatic route generation from OSM data (2015) and beauty-optimized routes (2016), the RUNAMIC system for dynamic personalized city routes with POI preferences and mid-run rerouting (2018), a recommender framework explicitly listing elevation, street lighting, weather, and time of day as route factors (ComplexRec 2018), and a 2024 IISE linear programming formulation optimizing closed-loop routes for distance, elevation, and route friendliness. [verified] (Source: ETH Zurich, "On the Fly! Automatic Running Route Generation," https://pub.tik.ee.ethz.ch/students/2015-HS/BA-2015-18.pdf, accessed 2026-07-30; ETH Zurich, "Smart Running Route Generation," https://pub.tik.ee.ethz.ch/students/2016-FS/MA-2016-51.pdf, accessed 2026-07-30; SciTePress, "RUNAMIC: Dynamic Generation of Personalized Running Routes," https://www.scitepress.org/Papers/2018/68896/68896.pdf, accessed 2026-07-30; Interactive Systems, "Recommending Running Routes: Framework and Demonstrator," https://interactivesystems.info/system/pdfs/858/original/ComplexRec-18-Final.pdf, accessed 2026-07-30; IISE, "A Linear Programming Application for Generating Running Routes Based on Individual Preferences," https://doi.org/10.21872/2024iise_6054, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Commercially, GraphHopper's hosted API and consumer apps such as komoot offer round-trip route planning for outdoor sports, but as trip-planning tools rather than coaching-integrated, runner-state-aware route generation. [inferred; the products exist (verified via GraphHopper docs above) but the gap characterization is my analysis]

Why it matters to Waypoint: the algorithms are published and the data is open, so the moat is not the router; it is the personalization layer (runner state, weather, safety, coaching integration) and execution quality, and fast followers are possible.

Classification: both.

## 5. Consumer running culture

What is happening:

- Global running participation grew from 672 million people in 2022 to nearly 785 million in 2025 (GWI data), more than 110 million new runners in three years. Gen Z runners grew from 186 million to 259 million over the same period. [verified] (Source: GWI data via Samba Digital, "The Running Boom Is Social," https://sambadigital.com/the-running-boom-is-social-how-digital-platforms-and-gen-z-are-reshaping-the-sport/, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Strava's Year in Sport 2025 (published December 3, 2025; 180+ million registered users) reports new clubs nearly quadrupled in 2025 to 1 million total clubs, running clubs grew 3.5x, club-organized events rose 1.5x, and Gen Z is 75 percent more likely than Gen X to cite a race or event as their main exercise motivation. [verified] (Source: Strava Press, "Strava Releases 12th Annual Year in Sport Trend Report," https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-releases-12th-annual-year-in-sport-trend-report-2025, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Race demand is at record levels: the 2026 London Marathon ballot drew 1,133,813 applications (a world record), with a near-equal gender split and over a third of entrants aged 18 to 29. [verified] (Source: Floom Creative, "The UK Running Boom 2026," https://floomcreative.co.uk/uk-running-boom-2026/, accessed 2026-07-30; Samba Digital article cited above.)
- The culture is "softer" and social: 37 percent of Gen Z describe exercise as their new way to socialize, replacing drinking culture; run crews are repeatedly cited as giving women safety and camaraderie; and Strava frames the 2025 trend as young people logging off social media and lacing up. [verified] (Source: Floom Creative article and Strava Year in Sport press release cited above, accessed 2026-07-30.)
- Travel running: Strava's 2025 report says members are putting movement first "even while on vacation," but publishes no granular vacation-running split; hard data on travel running behavior is thin. [verified that the claim exists; the size of the behavior is unquantified] (Source: Strava Year in Sport press release cited above, accessed 2026-07-30.)

Why it matters to Waypoint: the fastest-growing runner cohort is young, race-motivated, community-oriented, and safety-conscious, which maps directly onto adaptive routes (safety and crossing constraints, shareable club routes, race-build integration) and onto travel use ("where should I run from this hotel"). The social dimension is also a dependency: a solo route generator must eventually connect to the club layer where Strava is entrenched.

Classification: tailwind, with a social-layer dependency risk.
