# Founder Brief

Version-Timestamp: 2026-07-30 13:40:00 UTC-4

Captured in a structured founder discovery interview with Claudio on 2026-07-30. This is the founder's vision as stated, before research. Every hypothesis below is testable; the research phases exist to confirm or kill them, not to rubber-stamp them. Phase 4 (`research/04-synthesis/hypotheses-review.md`) delivers the verdict on each one.

## The core problem (founder's words, paraphrased)

Runners in specific situations, such as vacations, travel, or other unexpected scenarios, do not know **where to run**, and do not know how to obtain a good routine based on their own bodies, schedules, and needs.

Two intertwined needs:

1. **Where to run**: an unfamiliar city, a new neighborhood, a resort, a business trip. The runner has no local knowledge and existing tools do not answer "give me a good run from here, right now, for me."
2. **A routine built for me**: not a generic plan, but one shaped by the runner's body, schedule, and needs.

## The differentiation hunch

**Adaptive route generation** that adapts to the runner's needs and constraints: distance, elevation, humidity and weather, street crossings, and other constraints (safety, surface, traffic, time of day).

This is the wedge and the hero feature. Coaching and routines layer on top of it. The name Waypoint reflects this: the product's atomic unit is the route, not the plan.

## Target user (first core user, to be validated)

**The committed amateur**: runs 3 to 5 times per week, races a few times a year (5K to marathon), wants to improve, will not pay for a human coach.

## Product scope for v1

- Route generation is the wedge and hero feature.
- Coaching and adaptive routines layer on top of the route engine.
- Form factor: native mobile app, iOS first, with wearable integrations.

## Business intent

- **Ambition**: venture-scale. The Business Blueprint is literally the fundraising instrument.
- **Monetization instinct**: freemium subscription. Free basic routes; paid tier for adaptive coaching and advanced constraints. Phase 6 pressure-tests this.
- **12-month success criteria**: MVP live, early traction (thousands of active runners), and a seed round closed or in motion.

## Constraints

- Solo founder building from scratch: idea, design, development, launch, maintenance.
- Engineering mantra applies to everything shipped: security, stability, reliability, compliance. Health and location data make privacy compliance first-class, not an afterthought.

## Testable hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Tested in |
|----|-----------|-----------|
| H1 | "Where do I run" friction is frequent and painful enough for committed amateurs (especially when traveling) to drive app adoption | Phase 3 (user research) |
| H2 | No incumbent adequately solves constraint-based adaptive route generation (distance, elevation, weather, crossings, safety, surface) | Phase 2 (competitors) |
| H3 | Route generation is a stronger acquisition wedge than yet another training plan product | Phases 2 to 4 |
| H4 | Committed amateurs will pay a freemium subscription where adaptive routes plus coaching are the paid tier | Phases 1 and 6 |
| H5 | The addressable market is venture-scale (large enough to support an investor story) | Phase 1 (market) |
| H6 | Mobile-first iOS with wearable integration is the right v1 form factor | Phases 3 and 5 |
| H7 | Personalized routine building (body, schedule, needs) is a strong secondary need that deepens retention after the route wedge acquires the user | Phase 3 |
