## What is Waypoint?

**Your AI Running Coach.** An AI running coach whose hero feature is **adaptive route generation**: it answers "where should I run, right now, from here, for me" by generating routes that adapt to the runner's constraints (distance, elevation, weather and humidity, street crossings, safety, surface), then layers personalized coaching and routines on top of that route engine.

## Problem it solves

Runners in unfamiliar or unexpected situations (vacations, travel, new cities, changed schedules) do not know where to run, and do not know how to build a good routine based on their own bodies, schedules, and needs. Existing apps offer generic plans and static route libraries; none generate the right run for this runner, here, now.

## Audience

First core user (to be validated in Phase 3): the **committed amateur**. Runs 3 to 5 times per week, races a few times a year (5K to marathon), wants to improve, will not pay for a human coach.

## Success criteria

- 12 months out: MVP live, early traction (thousands of active runners), seed round closed or in motion.
- The Business Blueprint (Phase 8) serves as the fundraising instrument.
- Monetization hypothesis: freemium subscription (free basic routes; paid adaptive coaching and advanced constraints).

## Constraints

- Solo founder (Claudio) building from scratch: idea, design, development, launch, maintenance.
- Engineering mantra applies to everything shipped: security, stability, reliability, compliance. Location plus health data makes privacy compliance first-class.
- v1 form factor: native mobile app, iOS first, wearable integrations.
