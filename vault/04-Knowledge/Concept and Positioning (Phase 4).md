# Concept and Positioning (Phase 4)

Distilled from `research/04-synthesis/` (4 documents). Concept LOCKED by Claudio 2026-07-30 ([[DEC-006 Concept lock route-first positioning]]).

## The locked concept

Waypoint is the running app that knows where you should run. Hero: constraint-based adaptive route generation (distance, elevation, weather, crossings, surface, safety-awareness, training state as routing inputs). Paid layer: adaptive coaching connecting training state to route choice. Execution: voice-guided navigation, Apple Watch companion, share to Strava. Day-one architecture: privacy zones, private-by-default, the 15-item compliance checklist. NOT: social network, route library, multi-sport, hardware, plan brand war.

## Locked positioning and tagline

- **Positioning (Candidate A, route-first)**: "For committed amateur runners who never quite know where today's run should go, Waypoint is the running app that generates the right route for you, right now, from wherever you stand."
- **Tagline**: "Know where to run" (retired "Your AI Running Coach": it priced Waypoint against free Garmin/Apple and branded it against Runna).
- **Category strategy**: position inside the running app market ("the running app that knows where you should run"); "route intelligence" is investor vocabulary only.
- **H1 reframe accepted**: travel = activation moment and demo story; safety plus home novelty = daily retention wedge.

## The opportunity spine (for the blueprint)

Empty intersection (training apps have no location intelligence, route apps have no training context) + strongest-evidence unmet needs (safety-aware routing, route novelty) + near-zero-marginal-cost enabling stack + HealthKit as the one open personalization pathway + a 12 to 18 month window before Strava assembles the same capability. The investor story must carry the expansion path (H5): iOS English-first, then Android, geographies, and the same route engine pointed at walking, cycling, hiking.

## Material update from this phase

Strava Instant Workouts (January 2026) already attaches basic heatmap routes to suggested workouts. Differentiation rests on constraint depth, personalization, safety-awareness, and training-state integration. Added to the early-warning monitoring list.
