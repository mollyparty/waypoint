# User Research Key Findings (Phase 3)

Distilled from `research/03-users/` (5 documents, full citations there). Completed 2026-07-30. All desk research; the interview backlog in `unmet-needs.md` is the validation plan.

## The strategic reframe (most important finding of the phase)

**H1 verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED.** Travel route friction is real and constant (LetsRun threads spanning decades, r/running FAQ infrastructure, Great Runs existing solely to answer it), but the workaround economy mostly succeeds in 15 to 20 minutes and nobody in mined threads asked to pay for generated routes. The desk evidence points to a reframe for Phase 4:

- **Travel = activation moment and demo story** (the sharpest, most explainable instance of the problem).
- **Safety plus home novelty = the daily retention wedge** (3 to 5 times per week jobs).

## Top unmet needs by evidence strength

1. **Safety-aware routing (strong)**: 54 percent of women runners changed routes after incidents or fear; 42 percent say safety dictates their routine; 92 percent report safety concerns (Adidas 9k-runner survey); Strava's generator has documented dangerous outputs with no flagging. Zero of 15+ products accept safety as a routing input.
2. **Route novelty and personalization at home (strong)**: emotionally loud forum testimony plus CityStrides (90k+ users, 6.7M completed streets) proving paying demand for "roads not yet run". Incumbents rank by popularity, the exact opposite.
3. **Training-state-to-route connection (strong gap, inferred demand)**: total market absence, but no community post asks for it in those words; needs interview confirmation.

## Segments and personas

- **Primary: committed amateur** (confirmed with nuance: segment is flat, not growing, but it is the only proven payer). **Secondary: ambitious beginner** (Gen Z growth pipeline; 26 percent of Runna's base). Same product, gentler calibration; not a separate SKU.
- Personas: **Marcus** (committed amateur racer, bored of his three loops), **Priya** (traveling consultant, hotel lobby at 6 a.m. with 10 miles to do), **Elena** (safety-first city runner; the route IS the safety decision), **Jake** (ambitious beginner, one aggressive plan away from shin splints).

## Recommended main job

"When it is time to run and my usual route does not fit today (a new city, a dark morning, a heat wave, a tempo workout, or plain boredom with my loops), I want to be handed the right route for me, right now, from here, so I can spend my energy running instead of planning and worrying."

Untested risk: whether runners experience these situations as one job at all (the "bundling test" leads the interview backlog).

## Interview backlog (top priorities)

1. Committed amateurs (8 to 12, at least 4 monthly travelers): H1 base rate, bundling test, willingness to pay vs the free heatmap workflow.
2. Women runners (4 to 6, specialist moderation): can an algorithmic safety claim earn trust at all; a wrong answer is product-killing liability.
3. Frequent travelers (3 to 5): the night-before ritual, failure frequency, value of deleting it.

## Open founder decision

Whether safety-critical routing must be free (ethics and positioning). Needs a DEC record before Phase 6 pricing. #open-question
